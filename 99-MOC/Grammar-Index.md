## 口语时态与语气

详见 `03-Grammar/口语时态与语气.md`（40 个语法点），核心内容：

### 过去时 vs 现在时
- `should've + 过去分词` = 本该做却没做（S01E01）
- `was supposed to + 动词原形` = 过去计划但未实现（S01E01）
- `mentioned asking` = 过去时 + 动名词（S01E24）
- `I guess I figured you would understand.` = 过去时叠加表"原以为"（S02E11）
- `When it started, I was just trying to be nice.` = 叙事过去时 + 自我弱化（S02E02）
- `I missed the first time of everything.` = 过去时追忆遗憾（S02E20）

### 现在完成时 / 完成进行时
- `I've been in love with you since...` = 从过去延续至今的状态（S01E24）
- `I've never had...` = 人生经历（S01E07）
- `I've said... a million times` = 完成时 + 夸张数量（S01E19）
- `I've been doing this 10 years and I haven't gotten anywhere.` = 完成进行时 + 一事无成（S02E10）
- `That's the first time we've said that!` = 完成时 + 首次（S02E20）
- `I've already lost a fake dad...` = 完成时 + 反义对比（S02E09）

### 虚拟语气 / 条件句
- `If I were... I would...` = 第二条件句/与事实相反（S01E04）
- `What would you do if you were...?` = 假设疑问（S01E04）
- `if things were gonna happen... they would've happened` = 混合条件句（S01E19）
- `if you're gonna break his heart, that can wait` = 条件句的委婉劝阻（S01E24）
- `If it were me, I'd have difficulty getting out of bed.` = 第二条件句（S02E07）
- `If things were the other way around...` = 混合条件句（S02E08）
- `What if I found her... but I dumped her...?` = what if + 过去时假设（S02E03）
- `Say you do that.` = 祈使式假设（S02E02）

### 情态动词
- `Must mock Joey.` = 自发冲动式"必须"（S01E10）
- `Might want to...` = 委婉建议（S01E10）
- `Could've said...` = 本可以但没做（S01E07）
- `Don't you dare...` = 情态威胁句（S01E14）
- `Someone must have stolen my credit card.` = must have + 过去分词推测过去（S02E02）
- `I was wondering if we could grab a cup of coffee?` = 委婉请求（S02E02）
- `You might wanna consider wearing underwear.` = might wanna 委婉建议（S02E10）
- `You should have just said so.` = should have + 过去分词责备（S02E14）

### 关系从句 / 嵌入式问句
- `the guy that asked...` = that 引导定语从句（S01E19）
- `something you love doesn't love you back` = 嵌套从句（S01E10）
- `What do you think X is like?` = 嵌入式疑问句（S01E04）
- `All I asked you to do was...` = 伪分裂句（S01E19）
- `If you wanna know what the deal is...` = 嵌入式问句陈述语序（S02E07）
- `Do you have any idea how high up this thing goes?` = 嵌入式 how 问句（S02E12）
- `Ten percent of your paycheck goes where?` = 疑问词后置（S02E14）

## 口语句式与修辞

详见 `03-Grammar/口语句式与修辞.md`（37 个语法点），核心内容：

### 倒装表强调
- `are you in trouble!` = 感叹倒装（S01E01）
- `why the hell + 陈述语序` = 疑问词 + 强调（S01E24）
- `You are so going to...` = 现在进行时表注定（S01E10）
- `and so was I` = so 倒装承接/我也一样（S02E03）
- `Art it is.` = ___ it is 倒装拍板（S02E17）
- `only once in a blue moon does a dog's ear grow back` = only + 倒装（S02E21）

### 反意疑问句
- `You had sex, didn't you?` = 确认式反问（S01E01）
- `You're not laughing now, are you?` = 讽刺式反问（S01E04）
- `doesn't it seem like...?` = 寻求共鸣（S01E19）
- `I just gave away the ending, didn't I?` = 自我确认式反问（S02E01）
- `So it doesn't burn when you pee, does it?` = So + 转折式反问（S02E08）
- `We don't have to decide anything right now, do we?` = 拖延式反问（S02E15）

### 伪分裂句 / 强调句
- `It's more of a... kind of...` = 性质归类（S01E01）
- `What's an elf to do?` = 自嘲式伪分裂句（S01E10）
- `All I asked... was...` = 聚焦唯一要求（S01E19）
- `All it really was was just meaningless, animal sex.` = all...was 伪分裂句（S02E18）
- `All I can think of is that...` = all...is 伪分裂句（S02E11）
- `There is nothing sexier than...` = 最高级语义倒置/反话强调（S02E04）

### 排比 / 重复
- `small, tiny, petite, wee` = 同义词连珠炮（S01E24）
- `either A or B or C... and I'm...` = 排比 + 反转（S01E04）
- `day and night. And night and day.` = 倒序重复（S01E10）
- `I would wait... and wait... Then I'd wait some more.` = 重复递进（S02E04）
- `You work and you work and you work at a marriage.` = 动词三连重复（S02E22）
- `We have tenderness, we have intimacy, we connect.` = 三短句排比递进（S02E18）
- `She cried, I cried. She threw things, they hit me.` = 对仗排比（S02E08）

### 反语 / 轻描淡写
- `Just a small tranquilizer.` = 轻描淡写（S01E19）
- `I could. But I won't.` = 三词对仗式冷拒（S01E19）
- `Gum would be perfection?` = 过度修饰的反讽（S01E07）
- `It's just a little shorter than what we had discussed.` = 轻描淡写（S02E01）
- `Did my back hurt your knife?` = 反讽回击（S02E02）
